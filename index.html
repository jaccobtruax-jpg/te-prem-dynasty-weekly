FANTASY TIP OF THE WEEKWEEK 1 · KICKOFF EDITION
Week 1 is a liar.
Three friendly ground rules for the most overreacted-to Sunday of the year.
1
Start your studs. You spent real draft capital on these guys. One quiet half is not a reason to bench your first-round pick for the waiver flavor of the week. The Fantasy Footballers preach this every September, and every September they're right.
2
One game is one game. The season's worst trade gets made next Tuesday, when somebody ships a stud off the back of one bad Sunday. Week 1 is the smallest sample you will ever overreact to. Sit on your hands. By Week 4 you'll actually know what you have.
3
The UDK still counts. Every house line in this issue was built from a summer of projections and rankings, not a mood. Your guy posts a dud and the instinct is to burn the model down. Don't. It was right before kickoff, and one game didn't make it wrong.
Heard on the Fantasy Footballers podcast, cross-referenced with the UDK, and translated for this league. This spot runs every week now, sometimes general and sometimes very this-league: how the wire actually runs here, when a taxi call-up earns its spot, and what to do when your Monday-night starter is a game-time scratch. On that last one, no auto-subs are coming to save you. Sunday morning is your friend.
Every record in this league is 0-0 and it's never going to be that clean again. The startup is done (23 rounds, back in August), the taxi squads are full of rookies who spent the preseason scoring points, and real football finally starts tonight: the Super Bowl rematch nobody asked for, New England at Seattle. Niners-Rams is Thursday, the full Sunday slate follows, and the Chiefs-Broncos close Week 1 on Monday night. This issue has the five matchups, every piece of wire news that touches a drafted player, and the preseason edition of the rookie radar. No results yet. No awards. Just the edge-of-the-seat part.
